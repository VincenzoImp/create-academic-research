# Cover Letter

## Venue And Paper

State the target venue, track, manuscript ID, release ID, and submission system.

## Contribution Summary

Summarize only claims already supported by the accepted frame, paper claim map,
citation map, and contribution reports.

## Compliance And Availability

Reference badge targets, release status, anonymization mode, and any constraints
from the submission checklist.

## Final Gate

The cover letter may not introduce claims that are absent from the manuscript
claim map or unresolved in the citation audit.
