# Research Agenda Contract

## Inputs

- `sota/gaps.md`
- `sota/sota-claim-ledger.csv`
- `survey/survey-claim-ledger.csv`
- `survey/final/`

## Opportunity Records

Every agenda item belongs in `research_agenda/opportunity-ledger.csv`. Do not keep durable opportunities only as prose.

## Agenda Review Gate

Review each opportunity for novelty, feasibility, evidence, publishability, and ethical/release constraints before promoting it to contribution work.

## Direction Drafting

Use `research_agenda/directions/` for individual direction records and `research_agenda/final/` for the clean agenda synthesis.

## Decision Rule

Each opportunity must have a decision: pursue, watch, defer, reject, or needs-evidence. Rejected and deferred ideas remain useful if the failure condition and evidence gap are clear.
