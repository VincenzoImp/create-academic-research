# Research Agenda Workflow Prompt

## Purpose

Convert SOTA gaps and survey claims into prioritized, feasible, publishable, and
ethically sound research opportunities.

## Preflight

Run `npm run workflow:agenda` and inspect agenda contract, opportunity ledger,
SOTA gaps, SOTA claim ledger, survey claim ledger, survey final artifacts, and
agenda reviews.

## Required Skills

Use research-agenda, research-design-positioning, cs-methodology-evaluation, and
adversarial-peer-review skills.

## Ledger

Update `research_agenda/opportunity-ledger.csv`, direction records, decision
rationales, and agenda reviews.

## Review Loop

Assess each opportunity for novelty, feasibility, evidence, expected
contribution, failure condition, risks, cost, publishability, and release
constraints. Repeat review and fixes until no blocking issue remains.

## Handoff

Handoff only decided opportunities with clear contribution hypotheses,
evaluation paths, failure conditions, and known risks.
